Open access Environmental Research Letters · Vol. 20, 014001 · 2025

Quantifying food security and mitigation risks consequential to climate change impacts on crop yields

Hermen Luchtenbelt, Jonathan Doelman, Astrid Bos, Vassilis Daioglou, Jonas Jägermeyr, Christoph Müller, Elke Stehfest, Detlef van Vuuren

Using the IMAGE integrated assessment model with 120 climate–crop model combinations, this study quantifies how uncertain climate change impacts on crop yields translate into risks for global food security and land-based climate mitigation.

Featured project

Vogelradar

A map-first web & Android app that tracks rare bird sightings across the Netherlands in near real time, built on live data from waarneming.nl.

  • Interactive map of recent sightings, sorted by distance from your location
  • Filters for rarity level and time window
  • "Wish birds": pick species and get push notifications when one shows up nearby
  • Data refreshes automatically every hour
