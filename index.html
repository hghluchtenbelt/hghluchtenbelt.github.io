Featured project

Vogelradar 🦅

A map-first web & Android app that tracks rare bird sightings across the Netherlands in near real time, built on live data from waarneming.nl.

  • Interactive map of recent sightings, sorted by distance from your location
  • Filters for rarity level and time window
  • "Wish birds" — pick species and get push notifications when one shows up nearby
  • Data refreshes automatically every hour
FastAPI SQLite Leaflet Firebase Cloud Messaging Capacitor · Android
